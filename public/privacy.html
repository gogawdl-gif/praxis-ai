Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This policy explains what information Mobbilise collects when you use our website and training platform, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, work email, and company. When you use the platform to build training, we process the materials you provide, such as documents, screen recordings, and voice recordings, in order to generate lessons, simulations, and quizzes. We also collect basic usage data (pages visited, features used, device and browser type) to keep the product working and to improve it.

How we use information

  • To operate the platform, including generating and updating training content from what you provide
  • To authenticate your account and enforce access permissions your organization sets
  • To respond to support requests and communicate service updates
  • To improve reliability, performance, and the quality of generated training

We do not sell your data or your organization's training content to third parties, and we do not use your training materials to train models on behalf of other customers.

Data retention

We retain account and training data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below; Enterprise plans may configure custom retention periods.

Data security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to customer data is restricted to personnel who need it to operate or support the service, and administrative actions are logged.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to remember basic preferences. We use limited analytics cookies to understand product usage; you can control cookie behavior through your browser settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll notify account administrators by email.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@mobbilise.io.
